# Asset provenance and redistribution review

The aircraft, terminal, service-point and missile model sources are in `Assets/ORD/Models`. The aircraft was built as an original interpretation of a user-supplied Orion photograph. The photograph itself is not distributed. RC4 replaces the missile placeholder with an original authored game mesh generated by Tools/build_banderol_game.py. The available project files do not identify an external model or texture copied into these assets.

The addon references, but does not include, Arma Reforger base-game resources and the Workshop dependencies Propeller Flight Core (`69A8A34027DA65C5`) and ThermalPostProcessAssets (`69623A037A721B91`). Their own terms and continued availability are controlled by their publishers. The current resource scan has not established the licensing terms for every authored source asset or a redistributable license for this mod. Resolve ownership and license wording before Workshop publication.

No permission to republish the supplied reference photograph or dependency resources is asserted. The packaged addon must not contain those files.

RC6 engine audio is original procedural synthesis from Tools/build_orion_audio.py. No third-party audio recording was copied. The large explosion references native Arma resources; those resources are not redistributed.

RC8 uses the user-provided `Orion_SYNTHETIC_audio_illustration.zip` and `S8000_Banderol_SYNTHETIC_sound_pack.zip` as synthetic sound sources. The original archives are retained in `Integration/RC8/Audio/SourceArchives`. Additional gear, door and deployment cues are original synthetic additions. No sound is presented as an authentic aircraft or missile recording. The approved Orion and Banderol Blender masters remain independent in `AssetProduction/Orion_Banderol_R3_v001`; the game uses derived, editable integration copies. No supplied reference photograph is in the runtime package.
