## Marked wing-root bay correction

The user-marked dark projection was traced to `ORION_MAIN_R_BAY_SIDE_1`, a rectangular main-gear-bay liner extending through the tapered fuselage beside the right wing root. The corresponding left liner and related ends/roofs were clipped within the fuselage envelope. A conformal grey exterior skin closes the formerly exposed side on both sides; the original fuselage bay cutter, wing, gear and animation rig were left intact. The corrected editable checkpoint is `Integration/RC8/Blender/Orion_RC8_Motion_v020.blend`, with skinned and game-source checkpoints v004. `Integration/RC8/Reports/marked_bay_v020_id.png` is a neutral Blender view of the corrected area.

Blender sampled 479 half-frames against 144 moving gear/door meshes with **zero contacts** against the new side skins (`Integration/RC8/Reports/bay_skin_clearance_v020.json`). Workbench rebuilt 22 textures, 11 materials and both models with **35 successful builds and zero failed builds** (`Integration/RC8/Validation/MarkedBlockImport10/console.log`); `ORD_Orion_RC8.xob` changed after the import. The resource audit passed 122/122. The corrected runtime-only package was built in `Integration/RC8/Validation/MarkedFixPackage01`; the integrity checker found 123 exact source/PAK matches among 190 resources and no temporary handler. A rendered game test then captured the right fuselage/wing-root area with gear down, midway and retracted (`ValidationEvidence/RC8/MarkedBay_*`). The previously protruding dark triangular wall is absent in these views. That test used a camera/pose fixture excluded from the package; it does not prove gameplay-driven animation timing.

The v020 motion and v004 game checkpoints reopened in fresh Blender 5.2.2 processes. The pre-fix native-resource backup is preserved outside the addon tree at `C:\Users\david\Desktop\RC8_NativeAssets_PreMarkedFix_v001` to avoid duplicate resource IDs during Workbench import.

## Grey wing-root paint correction

The v020 conformal patch appeared too light in the game because its export fell into the equipment material group, whose Enfusion tint differs from the airframe. The revised `Orion_RC8_Motion_v022.blend` gives the patch its own paint group with the fuselage's grey source color. Ray-projected baking of this thin coincident shell initially produced black color/roughness maps, so `Orion_RC8_Game_v010.blend` contains a direct paint and tangent-normal bake on the patch's own UV atlas. The dominant patch base-color texel is `(134,142,147)`, matching the fuselage grey texel; roughness is 117/255 and the direct normal is neutral `(128,128,255)`.

The installed Enfusion patch material uses a tuned neutral tint of `0.55` under the captured daylight setup. The final rendered review is `ValidationEvidence/RC8/RC8_BayGrey_{Down,Transit,Retracted}.bmp.bmp`; the marked section is grey and no longer the white or black block seen in intermediate tests. Some natural brightness variation remains across its curved side and the wing's shadow. This is a material correction, not a change to the gear/bay geometry or flight behavior. Workbench rebuilt the final textures and model with 38 successful resource builds and zero failed builds (`Integration/RC8/Validation/BayColorImport04`), then rebuilt the final paint material successfully (`Integration/RC8/Validation/BayPaintTintImport03`). The resource audit passed **131/131**. The clean package in `Integration/RC8/Validation/BayGreyPackage01` contains 193 resources; 126 checked resources exactly match their source files, with no temporary import or camera handlers. The in-game captures used an isolated camera/pose fixture and do not establish live gameplay or multiplayer acceptance.
