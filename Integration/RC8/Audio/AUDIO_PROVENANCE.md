# RC8 synthetic audio sources

The two source archives were supplied by the user and are preserved byte-for-byte. Their bundled scripts are retained as source material, not executed. No supplied sound is represented as an authentic aircraft or missile recording. Stereo flybys, montages and combined incoming/impact demonstrations are preview material and excluded from live emitter candidates.

Gear motor, door latch and wing deployment cues are original procedural game sounds authored for this integration. Numerical PCM checks are not listening tests. ACP routing, spatial attenuation, operator-feed mixing and in-engine validation remain pending.
