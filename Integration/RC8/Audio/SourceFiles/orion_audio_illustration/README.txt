ORION-STYLE SYNTHETIC AUDIO ILLUSTRATION
======================================

IMPORTANT: NONE OF THESE FILES IS A RECORDING OF AN ORION DRONE.
This is an original, procedurally synthesized piston/propeller sound-design
mock-up. It has not been matched to verified Orion audio. Do not use it as
evidence of the actual aircraft's sound, pitch, loudness, startup timing,
propeller speed, operating parameters, or acoustic signature.

Created from mathematical waveforms and filtered noise; no third-party
recorded audio is included. No music, speech, combat sounds, or UI effects.
